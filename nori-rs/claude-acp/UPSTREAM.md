# claude-code-acp (vendored)

Vendored from https://github.com/soddygo/claude-code-acp-rs (MIT), via the maintained fork at
`4d217a8d09f64e149a81db08f887ae08300feccf` of `~/Projects/claude-code-acp-rs` (branch `robust`). It is linked into Nori as a
